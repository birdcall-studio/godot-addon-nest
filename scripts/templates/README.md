## Installation

The add-on needs Godot 4.7.

1. Download `@NAME@-v<version>.zip` from the
   [latest release](https://github.com/@REPO@/releases/latest).
2. Unzip it at your project's root, so the add-on sits at `addons/@NAME@/`.
3. In Godot, open **Project > Project Settings > Plugins** and enable
   **@TITLE@**.

To update, close Godot, delete `addons/@NAME@/`, and unzip the new release in
its place.
